Kepos V1 MLP style directions
Neo Cozy Indie Console Soft Brutalist

Default face for a private P2P home.

These are not final screens. They are visual hypotheses for the same product loop: trusted people, home chat, treehole, DM, and QR invite. The best direction should feel young without turning Kepos into a generic social app. Selected default theme pair: Neo Cozy for light, Indie Console for dark.

Neo Cozy

A private room feeling: warm, personal, soft, and easy to reopen every day. Good for making P2P trust feel human instead of technical.

Light theme

Indie Console

A lobby feeling: playful, social, and close to co-play. It makes future game rooms feel native, but may be too game-forward for V1.

Dark theme

Soft Brutalist

A young social-tool feeling: bold, memorable, and direct. It can stand apart from Discord clones, but needs strong execution.

Most memorable, highest risk
